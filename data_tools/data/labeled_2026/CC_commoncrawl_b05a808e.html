Common Crawl form, LLM-labeled.

You can now arrange for your bulky waste to be collected from the city of Jena online. Before registering, please find out what actually belongs to bulky waste and name all bulky waste to be provided for collection, including quantity and type. (see information under 'Bulky waste') The maximum waiting time between registration and collection is 4 weeks. Alternatively, you can deliver the bulky waste yourself to the recycling centers free of charge. Please enter your details here. We will contact you by e-mail as soon as possible. Waste that does not belong to bulky waste will not be taken away! (The provision of such waste constitutes an administrative offense and may be punished)

You can upload photos of the bulky waste to be collected. This makes it easier for us to make an assessment.
